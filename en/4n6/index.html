What the app contains

Time of death and thanatology

The Henssge nomogram with a corrective-factor assistant and 95 % intervals, post-mortem changes on a shared timeline (livor, rigor by muscle group, corneal clouding, Casper's rule), vitreous potassium by four methods with hypoxanthine as a check, and forensic entomology with the ADD method.

Toxicology

Ethanol: interpretation, Widmark, back-calculation to the time of the event, a blood-alcohol curve with an uncertainty band and breath conversion. Reference levels for 67 substances in therapeutic – toxic – lethal ranges and post-mortem femoral blood values from 122,000 autopsies. CO-Hb and vitality in fire.

Trauma and cardiac pathology

Burns (TBSA per Lund and Browder, the Baux score and its revision), body surface area and predicted heart weight, age from aortic circumferences, the Traube index, BMI and nutritional status.

Identification and anthropology

DVI per the INTERPOL DVI Guide 2023, odontology (dental chart, AM/PM comparison, six age-estimation methods), blood groups and stains, stature from long bones, sex and age of the skeleton, foetal age from the femur.

Microscopy and imaging

Cell and structure counting with semi-automatic detection, CPA quantification in desmin IHC, a grey-card-calibrated colorimeter and photogrammetry — lengths, angles and areas from a photograph, with native measurement on LiDAR iPhones.

Case and report

Enter the case data once; the tools hand their results to a shared report with sources, which you export and back up together with the case.

Built for expert practice

  • Every result carries a range or a caveat; an impossible input returns an explanation, not a number
  • A citation of the original source with every method, plus a unified reference list
  • Methods revised against the primary literature
  • Seven languages (SK, EN, CS, DE, IT, FR, UA) including recalculated results and the report, light and dark appearance

Frequently asked questions

How does 4n6 Tools Lite differ from Pro?

Lite is free and includes seven fully working tools: the Henssge nomogram, PMI from vitreous potassium, the Traub index, BMI, body surface area and predicted heart weight, age from aortic circumferences and a unit converter. Pro unlocks all 27 tools, including toxicology, ethanol, entomology, DVI, odontology and anthropology.

Is 4n6 Tools a medical device?

No. It is a professional aid for forensic pathologists and expert witnesses; values are orientational, every result carries a range or a caveat, and the final assessment belongs to a qualified expert.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All calculations run on the device; the app needs no connection and transmits no data.

Why does Pro cost €104.99?

It is an extensive professional tool: 27 tools, 67 substances with reference levels and methods revised against the primary literature, cited with every result. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription, ads or data collection; after VAT and the store commission roughly 57 % of the price reaches the author.

Which languages does the app support?

Pro (since 5.3) and Lite (since 5.0), in seven: Slovak, English, Czech, German, Italian, French and Ukrainian — the whole interface, results and report. On first launch it follows the phone language, and the language can be changed at any time with the header button.
