What Fotograme3ka 3D is for

The aPrehliadky app for iPhone and iPad can capture and reconstruct a 3D model directly on the device — but only on iPhones with LiDAR and an A17 Pro chip or newer, since Apple's iOS photogrammetry layer (CorePhotogrammetry) does not run on older devices or on a Mac. Fotograme3ka 3D is therefore a standalone, native macOS application that processes the same frame package through a native macOS photogrammetry framework — at a level of detail beyond what the iPhone itself can achieve.

A typical use case: an older iPhone without LiDAR, or without a sufficiently capable chip, can still capture in aPrehliadky, but cannot compute a model. The frame package is sent over AirDrop to a Mac, recomputed there, and the finished model returns to the iPhone as an .f3d package — including any findings and measurements carried over from the earlier model to the new one.

Higher detail

The iPhone computes the model only at low detail. The Mac offers medium, full and custom detail (a polygon cap, texture up to 16,384 px) — a 3 to 5 times finer surface for measuring findings.

Larger scans

The Mac limit is 1,500 frames versus 200 on the iPhone. Several packages dropped at once are processed in a batch, one after another.

Review and measurement

The app's second tab offers full-featured work with the finished model: orbit, zoom, measurement, findings marked directly on the skin, and a PDF report with certificate.

Findings transfer

After each recompute, camera poses are stored; on the next recompute they are fitted for yaw, scale and offset, and findings are carried over to the new model with an error on the order of millimetres.

How it works

  • Export the frame package from aPrehliadky on the iPhone (AirDrop to the Mac, or via Files)
  • Fotograme3ka 3D opens the package and starts the recompute at the chosen detail level — medium, full or custom
  • Progress, an estimated time and a thermal warning are reported throughout the computation
  • Review, measure and mark findings directly on the model on the Mac
  • Send the resulting .f3d package with its report back to the iPhone over AirDrop

No cloud, no upload

Fotograme3ka 3D runs inside the App Sandbox with no networking layer whatsoever — it can only access files you explicitly choose through the system Open/Save panel. The app never transmits models, images or reports anywhere; the only way data leaves the Mac is AirDrop or a file export you initiate yourself.

Fotograme3ka 3D is a companion to aPrehliadky, not a standalone product — without an .f3d package from aPrehliadky it has nothing to process. Both applications share the same package format and the same privacy principles.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Fotograme3ka 3D if my iPhone already has LiDAR and an A17 Pro chip?

Not necessarily. An iPhone with LiDAR and an A17 Pro chip or newer computes the model directly in aPrehliadky. Fotograme3ka 3D is useful when you want higher model detail (medium or full, a 3 to 5 times finer surface), or when you are capturing on an older iPhone without LiDAR or without a sufficiently capable chip.

How does the frame package get from the iPhone to Fotograme3ka on the Mac?

Via AirDrop. You export the frame package from aPrehliadky, open it in Fotograme3ka 3D on the Mac, the app recomputes it, and the finished model — including transferred findings and measurements — returns to the iPhone again as an .f3d package.

How many frames and what texture resolution does Fotograme3ka 3D support?

Up to 1,500 frames and textures up to 16,384 px, compared with a 200-frame and 8,192 px limit on the iPhone. Several packages dropped at once are processed in a batch, one after another.

Is Fotograme3ka 3D a paid app?

No, it is free. It requires a Mac with an Apple Silicon chip and macOS 15 or later.
